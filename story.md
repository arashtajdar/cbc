# 🌊 Welcome to Greenville: The Story of 4OUT Arena

Welcome to **Greenville**, a sun-drenched, incredibly cozy coastal town surrounded by rolling green hills, crystal-clear rivers, and endless sandy beaches. It’s a place where life moves at a relaxed pace, the weather is always perfect for a picnic, and everyone knows their neighbors.

But don't let the peaceful scenery fool you—Greenville has a massive competitive streak! The town's favorite pastime is the **4OUT Arcade League**, a friendly neighborhood championship tournament held in the local park's digital arena. It's a place where friends gather to play 10 chaotic minigames, test their reflexes, and earn the ultimate bragging rights.

Here are the stories of the four best friends who are ready to jump into the arena and claim the trophy!

---

## 🐻 1. Bumpo (The Cozy Bear)
* **Species:** Grizzly Bear
* **Personality:** Kind-hearted, laid-back, and highly motivated by snacks.

### Backstory
Bumpo is the sweet giant of Greenville. Most days, you can find him napping under the shade of a massive oak tree or organizing elaborate backyard barbecues for his friends. He isn’t one for drama, but he has an accidental habit of winning physical challenges just because of his sheer size and strength. 

### Why He’s in the Arena
Bumpo entered the 4OUT tournament for one simple reason: the grand prize is a lifetime supply of golden honey and custom picnic gear. While he moves a bit slower than the others, his heavy build makes him an absolute powerhouse in physical games like **BoxBrawl** and **SlideOut**.

---

## 🐿️ 2. Zippy (The High-Energy Squirrel)
* **Species:** Red Squirrel
* **Personality:** Hyperactive, enthusiastic, and impossibly fast.

### Backstory
Zippy is a ball of pure lightning. He works as Greenville’s chief mail carrier, a job he loves because it allows him to sprint across rooftops and leap over garden fences all day. He physically cannot sit still for more than three seconds, and he speaks almost as fast as he runs.

### Why He’s in the Arena
Zippy treats the 4OUT Arena as the ultimate playground to burn off his endless energy. He loves the thrill of the chase and the satisfaction of out-speeding his friends. His unmatched agility makes him a massive threat in survival and movement-heavy games like **HexCollapse** and **Sweeper**.

---

## 🦫 3. Puddle (The Creative Beaver)
* **Species:** River Beaver
* **Personality:** Resourceful, down-to-earth, and a bit clumsy on dry land.

### Backstory
Puddle is Greenville's resident architect and craftsman. If a bridge needs fixing or a treehouse needs building, Puddle is on the job with his tool belt ready. He's incredibly practical and loves analyzing how things work, though he occasionally trips over his own flat tail when he gets too excited.

### Why He’s in the Arena
Puddle enters the tournament to show off his spatial engineering skills and tactical thinking. He treats the games like structural puzzles, calculating the perfect angles to trap his opponents. His calculated approach makes him an expert in strategic territory games like **BounceClaim** and **Deflecto**.

---

## 🦝 4. Sly (The Clever Raccoon)
* **Species:** Striped Raccoon
* **Personality:** Witty, mischievous, and a master strategist.

### Backstory
Sly is a charming trickster who loves classic board games, arcade cabinets, and inventing overly complicated harmless pranks. He runs a vintage comic book and game shop in downtown Greenville. Sly always looks like he's hiding a secret, and he lives for friendly banter and good-natured rivalry.

### Why He’s in the Arena
For Sly, the 4OUT tournament is the ultimate chess match. He loves reading his friends' movements, setting bait, and capitalizing on their mistakes. His quick wits and tactical positioning make him incredibly dangerous in competitive combat arenas like **Ricochet** and **ShrinkZone**.

---

## 🎮 The Group Dynamic
These four are inseparable. Win or lose, they end every tournament day laughing over milkshakes and fries at the local beachside diner. They play hard, trash-talk constantly in the arena, but always have each other's backs when the game ends.
